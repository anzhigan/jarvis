Why nest the same Gantt? Consistency: a Step is structurally a mini-Sprint scoped to one Goal, with its own start/end window and own progress bar. Reusing the bar+axis primitive means the user reads "this thing has a window in time and a completion fill" exactly once. The dashed indigo frame around the inner Gantt signals nested context — you are now looking at one goal's internals.

Where step-less Gos live. Not every Go fits cleanly into a milestone — one-off chores, a single date-bound task, daily check-ins. They sit in the second (slate-dashed) strip below the step Gos, separated visually so it's obvious they aren't part of the timeline above. Same chip styling, same click target — only the container reads differently. Add new "loose" Gos from a `+ Add Go` button at the strip's right edge (omitted in this static mockup).
